**FOIA/declassification redaction codes:**

- **(b)(1)** — Information classified for national security reasons (national defense or foreign policy), per an executive order (currently E.O. 13526). This is the classic "classified information" exemption.
- **(b)(6)** — Personal privacy exemption: information that would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (names, personal identifying details of private individuals, etc.).

**NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") is a dissemination control marking, not a classification level itself — it indicates the information cannot be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or non-U.S. citizens, regardless of the document's classification level (Confidential/Secret/Top Secret).
