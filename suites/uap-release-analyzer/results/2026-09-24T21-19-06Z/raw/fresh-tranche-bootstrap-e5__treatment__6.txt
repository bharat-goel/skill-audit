Run `scripts/run_all.py <path-to-release_02>` — it chains inventory → extract_text → analyze → build_report in one shot and lands you a `REPORT.md`.

Two things to know going in:

1. **New filename prefixes.** If release 02 introduces prefixes not in `references/agency_vocab.md`, they'll fall into an `OTHER` bucket. If `OTHER` comes out above ~3% of files, add the new prefixes to `agency_vocab.md` and `PREFIX_RULES` in `inventory.py`/`analyze.py` before trusting the agency breakdown, then re-run.
2. **Timeouts on big PDF sections.** If you're running this in a sandboxed/45-second-call environment, `extract_text.py` can time out on the large FBI-style 200+ page files — call it with `[start] [end]` slice args in batches of ~25 instead of the single all-file call. Everything's idempotent, so you can chunk freely without redoing work.
