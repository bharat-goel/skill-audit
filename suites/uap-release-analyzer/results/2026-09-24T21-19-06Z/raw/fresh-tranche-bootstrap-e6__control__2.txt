Fastest path: bulk-extract text from everything in the tranche (OCR any scanned PDFs/images first via `ocrmypdf`/`pdftotext`), then fan the corpus out to parallel subagents that each read a chunk and write structured notes (facts, dates, entities, file/page citations) to disk, and finish with one synthesis pass that reads all those notes and drafts the report with citations back to source. The tradeoff: that parallel-summarize-then-synthesize approach is fast and scales to a large dump, but multi-agent summarization risks dropping or garbling details, so you want a final spot-check pass against primary source pages before treating the report as reliable.
