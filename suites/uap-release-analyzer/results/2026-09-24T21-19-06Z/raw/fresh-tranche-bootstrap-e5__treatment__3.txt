Once you've got release 02 downloaded locally, the fastest path is one command:

```
scripts/run_all.py <release_root>
```

That chains inventory → text extraction → analytics → report in sequence, writing `inventory.csv`, `text/`, `analytics/`, and `REPORT.md` under `<release_root>`. For a tranche the size of the last one (~160 files, thousands of pages), expect it to take a few minutes — it's incremental, so if a step times out or you rerun it, already-processed files are skipped.

Two things worth doing before you hit go:

1. **Check filename prefixes against `references/agency_vocab.md` first.** If release 02 introduces new source agencies or naming conventions, anything unmatched falls into `OTHER`, and once that bucket is above ~3% of files, the report is less useful. Better to extend the vocab (table + `PREFIX_RULES` in `inventory.py`/`analyze.py`) before running than to patch it after.
2. **If you're running this somewhere with a per-tool-call timeout**, use the chunked form of extraction instead of `run_all.py`'s all-at-once call: `scripts/extract_text.py <release_root> <start> <end>` in batches of ~25 files.
