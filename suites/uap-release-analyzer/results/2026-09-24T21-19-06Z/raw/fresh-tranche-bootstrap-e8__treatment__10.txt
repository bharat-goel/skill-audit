Fastest path once `release_02/` lands: run `scripts/run_all.py <release_root>`, which chains inventory → text extraction → analytics → report in one shot and writes `REPORT.md` at the end.

Two things worth doing alongside that: skip OCR by default (scanned/no-text-layer PDFs get flagged as gaps in the report's "What's missing" section rather than blocking you), and check the `OTHER` agency bucket in the resulting inventory — if unfamiliar filename prefixes show up (release 02 may introduce new agency naming), add them to `references/agency_vocab.md` and `PREFIX_RULES` in `inventory.py`/`analyze.py` before trusting the agency breakdown, since >3% `OTHER` means the vocab needs extending. If your environment has per-call timeouts, use `extract_text.py <release_root> <start> <end>` in chunks instead of the all-in-one runner for that step.
