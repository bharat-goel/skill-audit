Under FOIA (5 U.S.C. § 552), agencies cite specific exemption numbers when redacting material:

- **(b)(1)** — Information properly classified for national security reasons under an executive order (currently E.O. 13526), e.g., military plans, intelligence sources/methods, foreign relations.
- **(b)(6)** — Information that would constitute a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" if released, e.g., personnel/medical files, names, addresses, personal identifying details of private individuals.

**NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") is a dissemination control marking, separate from FOIA exemptions and classification levels themselves. It indicates the information may not be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or non-U.S. citizens, regardless of their clearance level.
