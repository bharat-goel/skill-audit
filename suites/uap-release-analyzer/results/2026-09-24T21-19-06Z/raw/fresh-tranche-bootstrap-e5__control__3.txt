For a first pass at a question like this, here's the shape I'd use: treat the tranche as a large, unstructured corpus rather than something to read start-to-front — split the files into batches and fan them out to parallel subagents (the `Explore` or `general-purpose` agent type) that each extract key facts, dates, names, and anything flagged as notable/redacted into structured notes, then do one final synthesis pass over those notes into the actual report. If any files are scanned images rather than text PDFs, OCR needs to happen before that fan-out step or the agents will just skim garbage.

The main tradeoff: the automated skim is fast but shallow, so it's worth budgeting a manual read-through of whatever the extraction pass flags as high-importance or ambiguous before you finalize the report — skipping that is where these pipelines quietly get things wrong (misread redactions, missed context between documents).
