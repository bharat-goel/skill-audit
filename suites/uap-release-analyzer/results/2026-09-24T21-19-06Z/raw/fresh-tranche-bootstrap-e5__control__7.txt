The fastest path depends heavily on what format the tranche actually comes in (scanned PDFs vs. structured data vs. mixed documents) — that determines whether you need OCR/extraction as a first step. In general the shape would be: (1) a script to pull/unzip and inventory the files, (2) an extraction pass to get everything into searchable text, (3) an LLM-assisted pass to summarize/tag each document and flag notable items, (4) a synthesis step that rolls those into a single written report with citations back to source files.

What does release 01 (or whatever the first tranche looked like) actually contain — PDFs, spreadsheets, images, a mix? That'll tell me whether step 2 needs real OCR work or is trivial, and whether it's worth building the pipeline now versus waiting to see release 02's actual structure.
