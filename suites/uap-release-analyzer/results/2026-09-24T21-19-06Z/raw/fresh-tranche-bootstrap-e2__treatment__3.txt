The fastest path: run `scripts/run_all.py <release_02_path>` — it chains inventory → text extraction → analytics → report in one shot.

Two things worth doing on the way in, since it's a new tranche:

- **Watch for new filename prefixes.** If the report's OTHER bucket comes out above ~3% of files, some of release 02's naming conventions aren't in `references/agency_vocab.md` yet — add the new prefixes there and to `PREFIX_RULES` in `inventory.py`/`analyze.py` before trusting the agency breakdown.
- **If your sandbox has a per-call timeout**, `run_all.py`'s extraction step can time out on the larger FBI sections (200+ pages). If that happens, fall back to calling `extract_text.py release_root <start> <end>` in chunks of ~25 pages instead of the all-in-one script — it's idempotent so you can pick up where a timeout left off.
