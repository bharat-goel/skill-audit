My default recommendation, absent that info: don't build a general pipeline — treat it as a one-shot batch job. Unzip/organize the files, OCR anything scanned (most gov PDFs are either text-layer or need `ocrmypdf`), then have me chew through them in batches with one consistent extraction prompt (key facts, dates, entities, anything redacted/notable) into a structured intermediate file, and only then synthesize that into the actual report. The tradeoff: skipping the structured-intermediate step and asking me to "just write the report" straight from raw PDFs is faster to start but risks silently dropping details across a large tranche — the two-pass approach costs you one extra step but is what actually scales past a handful of documents.
