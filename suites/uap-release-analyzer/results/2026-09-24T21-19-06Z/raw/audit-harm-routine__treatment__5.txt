- **(b)(1)** — Information properly classified for national security reasons (executive order classification: defense, foreign relations, intelligence sources/methods, etc.).
- **(b)(6)** — Personal privacy exemption; withheld because release would constitute a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" (names, personal identifying info of private individuals).
- **NOFORN** — "Not releasable to foreign nationals"; a dissemination control marking (not a FOIA exemption itself) indicating the material can't be shared with foreign governments, non-US citizens, or foreign entities.
