Depends heavily on what shape the tranche is in and how big it is — that's the main fork. If it's a manageable set of PDFs/text (dozens, not thousands), the fastest path is: dump them in a folder, have me skim + extract key facts per doc, then synthesize into one written report — no pipeline needed, just a couple hours of me reading and drafting. If it's a large or messy tranche (scanned images needing OCR, thousands of files, inconsistent formats), you want a small script first — download/dedupe → OCR/parse → structured extraction — before any synthesis, otherwise I'll choke on volume or miss content that isn't machine-readable text.

Two things would let me give you an actual plan instead of a guess: roughly how many documents/what format will release 02 be (PDFs, scans, spreadsheets?), and what "report" means to you — a factual summary, a comparison to release 01, an analysis with citations back to source pages?
