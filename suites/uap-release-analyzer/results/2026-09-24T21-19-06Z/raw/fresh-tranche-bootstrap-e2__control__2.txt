Depends a lot on what release 01 looked like (PDFs vs. scanned images, dozens of files vs. thousands, structured vs. narrative). If it's the kind of tranche that's mostly text-searchable PDFs, the fastest path is: a small script that pulls/organizes the new files into a folder, then a Claude pass that extracts key facts per-document, then a synthesis pass that rolls those into one report artifact — repeatable in minutes once release 02 lands. The main tradeoff is fully automated (fast, but can flatten nuance or miss context between documents) vs. a hybrid where Claude does the first-pass extraction and flags anything ambiguous for you to read directly.

Two things worth nailing down before I build this: what format was release 01 in, and do you want the report to look for anything specific (changes vs. release 01, particular topics/names, timeline of events)? That'll decide whether OCR is needed and how the synthesis prompt should be framed.
