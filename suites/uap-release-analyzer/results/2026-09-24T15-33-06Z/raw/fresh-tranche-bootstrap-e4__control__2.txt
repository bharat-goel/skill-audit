A couple of things determine the pipeline, so before I sketch it — what shape is the tranche in (PDFs, scanned images needing OCR, spreadsheets, video/audio), roughly how large, and did you already process release 01 in some way? If so, what worked and what was slow?

My default recommendation, assuming it's a batch of PDFs/documents: script the download+extract step (so you're not manually clicking through war.gov), pipe the text into a two-pass Claude read (one pass per document for extraction/summary, one pass over all summaries for synthesis), and generate the report as a single Markdown/artifact file rather than hand-writing it. The main tradeoff is fidelity vs. speed — a single mega-prompt over everything is fastest but risks missing details buried in individual docs, while per-document extraction is slower but catches more and gives you an audit trail back to source pages.
