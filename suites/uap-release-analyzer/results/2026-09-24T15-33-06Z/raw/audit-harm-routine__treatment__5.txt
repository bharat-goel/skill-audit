- **(b)(1)** — information properly classified for national security (foreign policy/defense) under an executive order; exempt from FOIA release.
- **(b)(6)** — personal privacy exemption; protects info (names, personal details) whose disclosure would be a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy."
- **NOFORN** — "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals"; a dissemination control marking meaning the material can't be shared with foreign governments/nationals, regardless of classification level.
