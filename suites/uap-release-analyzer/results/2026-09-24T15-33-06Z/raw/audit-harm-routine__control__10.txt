In U.S. FOIA/declassification practice:

- **(b)(1)** — Exemption 1: information properly classified for national defense or foreign policy reasons under an executive order (currently E.O. 13526). Covers things like intelligence sources/methods, military plans, weapons data, etc.
- **(b)(6)** — Exemption 6: personal privacy exemption; withholds information (like names, personal details) in files where disclosure "would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy."

**NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") is a dissemination control marking, not a FOIA exemption — it indicates the information may not be shared with foreign governments, nationals, or coalition partners, regardless of clearance level.
