- **(b)(1)** – Exempts information properly classified for national security under an executive order (e.g., military plans, intelligence sources/methods).
- **(b)(6)** – Exempts information that would constitute a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" (e.g., names, personal identifying details of private individuals).
- **NOFORN** – A dissemination control marking ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") indicating the information cannot be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or non-US-citizen personnel — separate from a classification level itself.
