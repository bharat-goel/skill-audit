- **(b)(1)** – Exemption for information properly classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy (executive order classified material).
- **(b)(6)** – Exemption protecting personal privacy: info in personnel/medical/similar files whose release "would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" (e.g., names, SSNs, home addresses of private individuals).
- **NOFORN** – "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals" — a dissemination control marking (not a FOIA exemption itself) indicating the classified info may not be shared with any foreign government, national, or coalition partner.
