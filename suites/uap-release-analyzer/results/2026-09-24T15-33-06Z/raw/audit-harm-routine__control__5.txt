These come from FOIA's statutory exemptions, not classification levels themselves, though they're commonly seen stamped on declassified/released documents:

- **(b)(1)** — Exemption 1: information properly classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy under an executive order (e.g., military plans, intelligence sources/methods).
- **(b)(6)** — Exemption 6: information that would constitute a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" if released — personnel, medical, or similar files (names, SSNs, personal details of private individuals).

**NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") is a dissemination control marking, not a classification level — it means the information cannot be shared with any foreign government, national, or international organization, regardless of the document's classification (Confidential/Secret/Top Secret).
