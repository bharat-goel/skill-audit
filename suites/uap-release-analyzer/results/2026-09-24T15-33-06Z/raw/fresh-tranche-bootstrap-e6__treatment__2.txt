Once release 02 lands in a folder, the whole pipeline is one command:

```
scripts/run_all.py <release_02_root>
```

That runs inventory → text extraction → analytics → report in sequence, writing `inventory.csv`, `text/`, `analytics/`, and `REPORT.md` into that folder. For a tranche the size of the May 2026 one (~4,000 pages), expect a few minutes.

Two things worth doing on the first pass with a *new* tranche, not automatic in `run_all.py`:

1. **Check the OTHER bucket in `inventory.csv`.** If new filename prefixes show up that aren't in `references/agency_vocab.md`, they'll fall into `OTHER`. If that's >~3% of files, it's a sign to extend the vocab (table + `PREFIX_RULES` in `inventory.py`/`analyze.py`) before trusting the agency breakdown in the report.
2. **OCR isn't run by default.** Scanned files with no text layer will show up as 0-char in `extract_text.py` and get flagged in the report's "What's missing" section — that's expected, not a bug. I won't run Tesseract on hundreds of scanned pages unless you ask.
