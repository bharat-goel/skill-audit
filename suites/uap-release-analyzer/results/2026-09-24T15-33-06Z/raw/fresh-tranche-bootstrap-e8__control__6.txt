Fastest path is a two-stage pipeline rather than dumping everything into one long context: (1) bulk-extract text from whatever format the tranche ships in (pdftotext/OCR for scanned PDFs, or straight parsing for structured data) into per-document plain-text files, then (2) have Claude process those in batches to pull structured findings (entities, dates, claims, citations back to source doc/page) before writing prose — synthesizing straight from raw extracted text tends to blur sourcing and miss items in a large corpus. Tradeoff: a single "just read it all and write me a report" pass is quicker to kick off but harder to verify and more failure-prone at volume; the extract-then-synthesize approach takes a bit more setup but gives you a report you can actually trust and cite.
