Two things worth knowing going in:

1. **New filename prefixes will likely show up.** If `OTHER` in the inventory ends up above ~3% of files, that's the signal to add the new prefixes to `references/agency_vocab.md` and `PREFIX_RULES` in `scripts/inventory.py`/`analyze.py` before trusting the agency breakdown — otherwise a chunk of the release gets miscounted.
2. **If you're running this somewhere with a ~45s per-call timeout**, `extract_text.py` can hit it on the FBI-style 200+ page files — pass it in batches with the optional `[start] [end]` args rather than backgrounding it.
