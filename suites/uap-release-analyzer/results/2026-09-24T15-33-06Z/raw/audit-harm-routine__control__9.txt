- **(b)(1)**: Withholds information properly classified for national security under an executive order (e.g., E.O. 13526) — the most common exemption for classified defense/intelligence material.
- **(b)(6)**: Withholds information that would constitute a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" if released — used to redact names, personal identifiers, and similar details about private individuals.
- **NOFORN**: "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals" — a dissemination control marking indicating the information may not be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or non-U.S. entities, regardless of clearance level.
