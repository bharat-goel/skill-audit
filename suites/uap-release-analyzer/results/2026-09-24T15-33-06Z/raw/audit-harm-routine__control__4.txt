- **(b)(1)** – Exempts information properly classified under an executive order for national security reasons (defense/foreign policy secrets). Applied under both FOIA and, in this context, similar exemption codes used in declassification review.
- **(b)(6)** – Exempts information that would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (names, personal identifying details of private individuals).
- **NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") – A classification/dissemination control marking indicating the information may not be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or non-U.S. citizens, regardless of clearance level.
