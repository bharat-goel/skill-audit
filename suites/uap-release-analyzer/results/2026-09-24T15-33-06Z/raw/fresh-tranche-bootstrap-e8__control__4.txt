For a document dump like that, fastest path is usually: **OCR/normalize the files first** (if any are scanned images rather than text PDFs), then **fan out to parallel subagents** — one per document or per batch of ~10 — to extract key facts/entities into a structured intermediate (bullet notes, not prose), and finally **one synthesis pass** that reads all the intermediates and writes the actual report. That keeps the "read everything" step parallelized (fast) while keeping the "write something coherent" step single-threaded (so it doesn't read like 40 people wrote it).

Main tradeoff: parallel-first is much faster for a large tranche but risks missing cross-document connections that only show up when one reader sees everything together — worth a targeted second pass (search for names/dates that recur across the per-doc notes) rather than trusting the synthesis step to catch it all on its own.
