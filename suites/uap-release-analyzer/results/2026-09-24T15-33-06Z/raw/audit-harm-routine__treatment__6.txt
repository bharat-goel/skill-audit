- **(b)(1)** — Information properly classified for national security reasons (executive order classification, e.g. SECRET/TOP SECRET material).
- **(b)(6)** — Personal privacy exemption: withheld to protect information that would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (names, personal identifiers of private individuals).
- **NOFORN** — "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals" — a dissemination control marking, not a FOIA exemption itself, indicating the material may not be shared with foreign governments/nationals regardless of classification level.
