- **(b)(1)** — information properly classified for national security reasons (executive order classification: defense, foreign relations, intelligence sources/methods, etc.).
- **(b)(6)** — personal privacy exemption; withholds info (names, personal details) whose disclosure "would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy."
- **NOFORN** — "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals"; a dissemination control marking (not a FOIA exemption itself) indicating the material cannot be shared with foreign governments, nationals, or coalition partners regardless of classification level.
