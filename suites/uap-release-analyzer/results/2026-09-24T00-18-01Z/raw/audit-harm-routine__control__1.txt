Under the Freedom of Information Act, exemption codes on redacted documents refer to 5 U.S.C. § 552(b):

- **(b)(1)** — Information properly classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy under an executive order (e.g., Top Secret/Secret/Confidential material).
- **(b)(6)** — Information that would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy (e.g., names, personal details of private individuals).

**NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") is a classification dissemination control marking indicating the information may not be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or foreign intelligence services, regardless of their clearance level — it's separate from the classification level itself (Confidential/Secret/Top Secret) and from the FOIA exemption codes used when documents are later released.
