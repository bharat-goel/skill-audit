In FOIA/declassification redaction markings:

- **(b)(1)** — Information properly classified for national security (per an executive order, currently E.O. 13526) — e.g., military plans, intelligence sources/methods, foreign relations. This is the exemption most often seen redacting substantive content in declassified national security documents.
- **(b)(6)** — Personal privacy exemption: information whose disclosure would be a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" — e.g., names, personal identifiers of private individuals mentioned in a document.

**NOFORN** ("Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals") is a dissemination control marking, not a FOIA exemption — it indicates the information cannot be shared with any foreign government, foreign national, or non-US-citizen, regardless of clearance level.
