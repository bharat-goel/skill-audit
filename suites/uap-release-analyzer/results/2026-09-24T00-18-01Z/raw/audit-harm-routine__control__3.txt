- **(b)(1)** — FOIA exemption for information properly classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy (i.e., classified national security information under an executive order).
- **(b)(6)** — FOIA exemption protecting personnel, medical, and similar files whose disclosure "would constitute a clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" (personal privacy exemption).
- **NOFORN** — "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals": a dissemination control marking indicating the information may not be shared with foreign governments, foreign nationals, or non-US citizens, regardless of clearance level.
