- **(b)(1)** — Exempts information properly classified in the interest of national defense or foreign policy (e.g., under an executive order like E.O. 13526). Common in military/intelligence records.
- **(b)(6)** — Exempts information that would constitute a "clearly unwarranted invasion of personal privacy" if released — names, personal identifiers, and similar details about private individuals.
- **NOFORN** — "Not Releasable to Foreign Nationals." A dissemination control marking (not a FOIA exemption itself) indicating the classified information cannot be shared with foreign governments, nationals, or organizations, only U.S. persons/entities with proper clearance.
