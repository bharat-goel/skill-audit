**Change I'd make regardless of version: put the full path in the trace exporter URL**

```go
exporter, err := otlptracehttp.New(ctx,
    otlptracehttp.WithEndpointURL("http://otel-gateway:4318/v1/traces"),
)
```

- `WithEndpointURL` takes a full URL, and the path in it is used as given. You want traces at `/v1/traces`, so this makes that explicit.
- The current form, `http://otel-gateway:4318`, leaves the path empty. Whether an empty path falls back to `/v1/traces` is exactly what could differ between releases.
- With the full path, the result is the same on v1.44.0 and v1.45.0, so the upgrade can't change it.
- The alternative is `WithEndpoint("otel-gateway:4318")` plus `WithInsecure()`, which leaves the default path in place.

**Retry-After: `Retry-After: 5` versus an HTTP-date**

I can't say whether the v1.45.0 exporter honors both forms, or how that differs from v1.44.0. The delta-seconds form (`5`) is the usual one for OTLP/HTTP exporters to support. HTTP-date support is what varies. The two forms differ like this:

- **`Retry-After: 5`**: the exporter waits 5 seconds.
- **`Retry-After: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 07:28:00 GMT`**: the exporter has to parse the date and wait until then. If it can't, it falls back to its own backoff.

To settle it, read the "Fixed" and "Changed" entries under 1.45.0 in the upstream `CHANGELOG.md`. Or grep the vendored `otlptracehttp` client, in `internal/retry` and `client.go`, for `Retry-After`. You can also write a local test with `httptest.NewServer` that returns 429 with each header form and records the retry timing. That needs no external endpoint.
