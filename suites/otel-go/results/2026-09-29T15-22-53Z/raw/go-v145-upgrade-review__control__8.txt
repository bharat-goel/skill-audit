**Changes I'd make, and how sure I am of each**
1. **Exporter endpoint (safe in any version).** Spell out the full path so you don't depend on whether the exporter appends `/v1/traces` for a bare URL:
   ```go
   exporter, err := otlptracehttp.New(ctx,
       otlptracehttp.WithEndpointURL("http://otel-gateway:4318/v1/traces"),
   )
   ```
   `WithEndpointURL` treats the URL as the full target. `WithEndpoint("otel-gateway:4318")` plus `WithInsecure()` is the form that gets the default path. Check that a bare-URL path isn't silently wrong in either version.
